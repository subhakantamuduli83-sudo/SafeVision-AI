📚 Regulatory Standards & Technical References

Authoritative industrial safety benchmarks and underlying edge computer vision neural architectures.

🛡️

1. Industrial Safety Standards & Regulatory Compliance (OSHA & NFPA)

Federal & international occupational health governance protocols enforced by SafeVision AI

• OSHA 1910 Subpart I (29 CFR 1910.132–138) — General Industry PPE General Industry Safety Standards for Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), defining mandatory head protection (helmets/hardhats), eye and face protection (goggles), high-visibility apparel (vests), hand protection (gloves), and protective footwear (safety shoes/boots).
• OSHA 1926 Subpart M & Subpart P — Construction Safety Safety and Health Regulations for Construction, governing fall protection harnesses and lifelines for elevated decks (29 CFR 1926.501) and excavation trench collapse setback margins (29 CFR 1926.651).
• NFPA 51B — Hot Work & Fire Prevention Standard for Fire Prevention During Welding, Cutting, and Other Hot Work, requiring fire safety watch and designated extinguisher readiness near sparks.
⚡

2. Computer Vision Models & Edge AI Acceleration

Deep learning vision backbones and hardware optimization runtimes

• Ultralytics YOLOv8 & YOLO11 Architecture Real-time object detection models for PPE and hazard identification, combined with YOLO11-Pose 17-keypoint skeletal tracking for worker slip, trip, and fall detection.
• Intel OpenVINO Toolkit Deep learning runtime and model optimization framework enabling sub-100ms CPU-accelerated inference on local edge devices without high-end server GPUs.
